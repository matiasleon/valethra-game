# Worldbuilding guidance

- `canon.md` contains the only established world canon.
- Do not promote names, factions, orders, religions, maps, dates, or cosmology from ideas to canon without explicit approval.
- Preserve Valethra's adult-fantasy tone: functional but unjust societies, morally costly choices, no inherently good or evil peoples.
- Record hypotheses and alternatives in `ideas.md`, clearly labeled as undecided.
- Keep gameplay examples from silently establishing global canon.
